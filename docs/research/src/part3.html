5iOS in depth

An App Store app has exactly one supported way to restrict other apps: Apple's Screen Time API (iOS 15+, extended every year) [20][21]. It needs no device management, but distributing it requires Apple's approval (12.1).

5.3 What ManagedSettings can restrict

An MVP needs only the shield group: applications, applicationCategories, webDomains and webDomainCategories [83]. Other groups exist (hiding apps [81], denying app removal [11], forcing automatic date and time [82]), but which of them work under individual authorization is undocumented; deny-removal is "not guaranteed" [26].

5.4 Shields and the picker

  • Block screen: title, subtitle, colours, an icon you supply and two buttons [12]. The extension is the only place the app sees the blocked app's name.
  • Buttons: close, defer or no action [14]; up to 3 menu items on 26.4 [13]; open the app that applied the shield on 26.5 [14].
  • Websites: covered in Safari and browsers that report through Screen Time [83][84]. Chrome, Firefox and Brave reportedly aren't Community.
  • Limits: 50 apps per list [10]; an allow list (.all(except:)) takes up to 50 exceptions [83]; system apps such as Phone and Settings can't be shielded [96]; the picker reportedly returns fresh tokens on every "Done" [101] Community.

5.7 Recent changes, and what iOS can't do

2025 to November 2026

  • 26.4: shield submenu [13]; EU-only usage data [5].
  • 26.5: open the app from the shield [14]; token expiry and store management [15].
  • March 2026: developer agreement primary-purpose rule (12.1) [23][24].
  • iOS 27: no new developer APIs; Apple added its own parental "Time Allowances" [33].
  • App Review Guidelines: no Screen Time-specific changes; 4.10 still applies [22].

Not possible on iOS

  • Turning on Do Not Disturb or Focus. There's no API, and Focus filters only react when the user turns a Focus on [32][86][87].
  • Reading usage minutes or app names (outside the EU).
  • MDM for consumers (enterprise and education only) [22][31].
  • Preventing an adult from revoking access or deleting the app.
