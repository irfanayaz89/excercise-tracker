The pattern: on iOS, only revoking access and deleting the app fully defeat a block, and a Screen Time passcode someone else holds can gate both. On Android, also stopping the app, Safe Mode, other profiles and ADB. Another device (iPad, laptop) is out of scope, since authorization is per device [21]. The app's answer is visibility (banner and history), not prevention.
