## C. Improvements made

- **Rewards:** 1 min per push-up equivalent, +5 min at 10 and 20, first-set bonus; 10 push-ups = 15 min, 20 = 30 min.
- **Calories and goals:** ranges from body weight, height, age, sex and body type; 30/60-day projections; targets per exercise.
- **Screen Time you can rely on:** clock changes, damaged storage, lost permissions and killed services are handled and explained.
- **Usability:** fixed Start bar, earn → guide in one tap, confirm dialogs, "Show more", data deletion.
- **Accessibility:** 44 pt targets, readable contrast, text scaling with sensible caps, grouped and announced live counts.
- **Performance:** no camera re-render per rep, far fewer Home database reads, animations pause off screen.
- **Code:** one type scale, one shared Stat, 3,191 tests.

### Still to run on a real iPhone (development build, paid team, `SCREEN_TIME=1`)

1. Move the clock forward and back during a lock, a focus, an unlock and Strict: shields hold and lift at the real time.
2. Take an emergency unlock and keep using the app past its end: it re-shields within about 2 minutes.
3. Earnable own-list lock: earned minutes run out, then it shields. An Everything lock stays shut.
4. Fresh install: permission asked once. Relaunch: not asked again.
5. Kill the monitor during use (Console): limits still apply that day.

### Screen reader pass (TalkBack 17, Android emulator)

Read from TalkBack's own speech log:

- **Home and Settings:** buttons say what they do; stats read as one item ("Used today: 0 min / 1 h"); exercise cards read "Push-ups. Counted reps. 0 push-ups in the last 7 days"; switches read their state ("ON. Voice coach. Switch").
- **Live set, voice coach off:** each rep read once ("5 reps, 4 clean") and each cue ("Hips sagging", "Go lower").
- **Found and fixed during the pass:**
  - Each rep was read twice: the per-rep announcement plus the count's label changing with the running timer. Now Android uses a live region, iPhone the announcement, and the label has no timer.
  - The 30/60-day card was one block labelled only with its title, which hid its figures and its link. It's now readable item by item.
  - Choice chips were read as plain buttons. They're now radio buttons ("checked. Regular. Radio button"), and checkboxes where several can be picked.
  - "›" arrows were read out. They're now dropped from speech.
  - "1 reps" now reads "1 rep", and trailing full stops are gone from labels.
- **Not run:** VoiceOver on a real iPhone.
