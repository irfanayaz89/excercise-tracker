# TrueLine UX/UI review — 26 Nov 2026

Reviewed in the iOS Simulator (iPhone 17 Pro) with real profiles and sets, against mobile UX guidance: progressive disclosure, one primary action per screen, content priority, readable data density.

**The main finding.** The app is honest and accurate, but it explains itself everywhere at once. Formulas, ranges, sources, fat equivalents and caveats appear inline on the screens where people make decisions. The fix is the same everywhere: one clear number, its range, and a link to the method.

## Fixed in this change

| Where | Problem | Change |
|---|---|---|
| **30/60-day card** | Each column stacked kcal, a range, workouts, grams of fat and a weight band, plus a 4-line paragraph and a link. Too many numbers to read at once. | One headline per column, picked by goal: weight for lose-fat, best set once there's a forecast, otherwise kcal. Under it, one "likely" range and the workout count. One footer: "Estimates, not promises · How it's worked out". The detail moved to the explainer screen. |
| **Home order** | The outlook card sat between the goal and Today, pushing what you did today and the exercises below the fold. | Order is now goal → Today → Screen Time → Exercises → outlook → Recent sets. The weekly line drops "about the energy in N g of body fat". |
| **Goal setup, step 1** | Four goal cards plus six optional "why" chips. | The chips are behind "+ Add what would make it worth it". |
| **Goal setup, step 2** | About three screens long: three tall activity cards, build, waist, and six full-width health cards. | Activity is a 3-way choice with a one-line hint. Build and waist are behind "+ Make the numbers more accurate" (open if already set). Health answers are short chips; screen readers still hear the full wording. |
| **Goal setup, step 3** | Three tall pace cards. | A 3-way Gentle / Steady / Faster choice with one hint ("0.5 kg a week: the usual advice"). The whole step now fits one screen. |
| **Goal setup, plan** | A big number plus two fact cards, a lasting-change paragraph, a "Take care" box, the outlook, and a paragraph naming equations and BMI. About 2.5 screens. | A big number, then a 3–4 row summary: "Your body uses / You lose about / Your workouts burn" (strength: Train / Protein / Eat / burn). Then the outlook and a one-line note. One screen. The formulas, sources and long-term effect are on the explainer screen. |

Nothing was removed outright. Every fact that left a screen is either one tap away (explainer, "+ more" sections) or already elsewhere.

## Recommended next (not changed yet)

Ordered by impact.

1. ~~**Exercise screen is long (11 sections).**~~ **Done:** camera view, phone, difficulty and focus are one "Set options" row ("Side-on · Landscape · Medium · Focus off") that opens to change them. The numbered setup checklist is gone from this screen; the guide already shows it before a set in guided mode.
2. ~~**Screen Time screen is a settings dump.**~~ **Done:** the main screen is Today, Earn time and five rows (Rules, Earning, Strictness, History, More), each with a one-line summary and opening its own view. A change that waits now shows once (the alert and its "Cancel the change" box), not three times.
3. ~~**Workout summary repeats coaching.**~~ **Done:** the result (count or time, tally, "17 clean · 2 flagged", calories), the personal best and one "Next time" tip come first, then the actions. The stats grid, tempo chart and form lists are behind "Rep details" / "Hold details". Planks and rep sets now follow the same order.
4. **Settings has 9 sections, including developer ones.** "Tuning" (record sets, tracking details) and model choice are for testing. Hide them behind a "Developer" row or show them only in debug builds.
5. **Two words for one thing.** "Goal" means both the profile goal (lose fat) and the daily goal (reps today). Suggest calling the daily one "Daily target" everywhere.
6. **Label style is overused.** Spaced uppercase mono labels head almost every block, including links ("HOW IT'S WORKED OUT ›"). Uppercase links read as headings. Suggest sentence-case links in the brand yellow, keeping uppercase for section titles only.
7. **The floating "Tools" button** in the Simulator screenshots is Expo's dev menu, not part of the app; ignore it when judging layouts.

## Checked and fine

- One primary action per screen (yellow button), and secondary actions are ghost buttons.
- Touch targets are 44 pt or larger; chips wrap instead of shrinking.
- Text scaling caps hold at the largest accessibility size (checked earlier).
- Destructive actions confirm; the safety guards (pregnancy, under 18, low weight) block weight loss with a clear alternative.
